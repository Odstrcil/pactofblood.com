Story

The world has fallen, consumed by blood magic. All that remains is a single survivor, protected by the quiet strength of an ancient magical barrier. It is the last safe place in a land where everything else has withered or turned hostile.

Survival now depends on a mysterious portal that awakens only from time to time. Each time it opens, it leads into a different, dangerous place filled with forgotten ruins, lurking threats, and echoes of a lost past. With every journey, there is a chance to gather precious resources and uncover fragments of the truth behind the world’s collapse.

Within the safety of the barrier, there is space to build, to grow, to prepare for what comes next. But the calm is never permanent, and the silence beyond the trees is never truly empty.

Pact of Blood is a dark medieval survival adventure with a strong focus on atmosphere, exploration, and storytelling. Its realistic visuals and slow, immersive pace draw players into a haunting world where every decision matters and every step brings new risks.

The portal is the only way forward.
The barrier is the only place to rest.
And the story waits to be found, piece by piece.

Key characteristics

•  Blood for Rewards – Sacrifice health at ancient altars in exchange for rare and powerful items.

•  Portals – Travel through both procedurally generated and handcrafted dimensions.

•  Ritual Runes – Change the destination of portals using special magical runes.

•  Survival System – Hunt, gather, craft, and build to stay alive.

•  Tactical Combat – Fight with swords, crossbows, bombs, spells, and traps.

•  Mutated Enemies – Face mythical creatures twisted by blood magic.
