Short description

Pact of Blood is a dark medieval survival game. Protected by a magic barrier, the last survivor explores dangerous places through portals that open for a short time. Build, craft, and uncover what destroyed the world. On the other side of the portal, blood is in high demand. By offering blood to ancient altars, special rewards can be gained. The question is, how much health will the survivor sacrifice?

About game

The world has collapsed under the power of blood magic. Now, only one person remains, protected by an ancient magical barrier – the last safe spot in a land where everything else has died or turned hostile.

Survival depends on a mysterious portal that opens from time to time, but only for a short while, leading to dangerous places filled with old ruins, hidden threats, and memories of a lost world. Within the barrier, it’s possible to build, craft, and prepare for what lies beyond. But the peace never lasts long, and the quiet forests around are never truly empty.

Pact of Blood is about atmosphere, exploration, and storytelling. It features realistic visuals and a slow, immersive style that draws players into its dark medieval world, where every choice matters and each step can bring new dangers.

Key features

• Blood for Rewards
Sacrifice health at ancient altars to gain rare and powerful items.

• Portals
Travel through both procedurally generated and handcrafted dimensions.

• Ritual Runes
Change portal destinations using special magical runes.

• Survival System
Hunt, gather, craft, and build to stay alive.

• Tactical Combat
Fight using swords, crossbows, bombs, spells, and traps.

• Mutated Enemies
Face mythical creatures twisted by blood magic.

Crowdfunding

The independent Czech-Slovak studio behind Pact of Blood is currently running a crowdfunding campaign on HitHit until July 18, 2025. This campaign is vital to complete the game with full polish and unlock exclusive additional content that will enrich the player experience.

Backers will receive exclusive rewards, including a rare opportunity to become part of the game’s story—your name or likeness will be permanently featured on characters, portraits, or statues within the game world.

By supporting Pact of Blood, backers directly contribute to bringing this ambitious indie project to life and helping it reach its full potential.
